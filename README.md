## Moustafa Hamwi · founder of ScoutHub Lab

I am a scout leader and software developer from Beirut, Lebanon. In 2021 I built a small tool for one scout troop that was buried in paperwork: Smart Scout Manager. The same problem was everywhere, so it grew into **[ScoutHub Lab](https://scouthub.dev/en/)**, a digital innovation lab in Beirut that builds Arabic-first tools for scouts, NGOs, education and digital safety.

Today: 20 shipped projects, used by more than 17,000 people in more than 12 countries.

### Projects

**Scouting**
- [Smart Scout Manager (SSM)](https://scouthub.dev/en/projects/ssm/): runs a scout troop or association in one place: members, activities, inventory, finances and reports.
- [Najem](https://scouthub.dev/en/projects/najem/): a free Telegram assistant that helps scout leaders plan activities and camps, in Arabic.
- [Scout Compass](https://scouthub.dev/en/projects/scoutway/): curriculum, badges, games and songs for leaders, in one guide.
- [ScoutQuest](https://scouthub.dev/en/projects/scoutquest/): an interactive Arabic scouting quiz game.
- [Masaar](https://scouthub.dev/en/projects/masaar/): turns an association's curriculum into a clear path for every scout.

**Digital safety**
- [Kashef](https://scouthub.dev/en/projects/kashef/): an Arabic-first website security audit with an A+ to F grade.
- [LinkGuard](https://github.com/hamwimoustafa88-maker/LinkGuard) (open source, MIT): checks a suspicious link against several threat sources in parallel and explains the verdict.

**Tools**
- [DropFlow](https://github.com/hamwimoustafa88-maker/dropflow) (open source, GPL-3.0): a file converter that runs entirely on your device.
- [eNaawah](https://github.com/hamwimoustafa88-maker/naawah) (open source, GPL-3.0): print-quality Arabic obituary notices, free.
- [Talqeen Dev](https://scouthub.dev/en/projects/talqeen/): an Arabic-first platform for learning to build software with AI.

We also design and build websites for NGOs in Lebanon and Türkiye. All projects: [scouthub.dev/en/projects](https://scouthub.dev/en/projects/)
